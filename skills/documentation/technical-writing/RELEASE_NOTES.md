# technical-writing 1.0.0

Version 1.0.0 gives style rules for all technical prose: short sentences, lists over prose, one idea per line, what and why on one line, no jargon or filler, a lexicon chosen by audience and short ADR/DEC decision records. It needs no other skill.

Acquire the whole `technical-writing` directory from `Tacuchi/agent-skills` once the ref `skill/technical-writing/v1.0.0` exists.
