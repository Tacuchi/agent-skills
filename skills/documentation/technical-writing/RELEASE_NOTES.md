# technical-writing 1.1.0

Gives style rules for all technical prose: short sentences, lists over prose, one idea per line, what and why on one line, no jargon or filler, a lexicon chosen by audience and short ADR/DEC decision records. It needs no other skill.

Version 1.1.0 adds one rule: compressing prose deletes more than intended, so after cutting, the whole text is read again.

Acquire the whole `technical-writing` directory from `Tacuchi/agent-skills` once the ref `skill/technical-writing/v1.1.0` exists.
