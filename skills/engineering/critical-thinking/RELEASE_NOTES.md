# critical-thinking 1.1.0

Publishes three short checks to run before acting on a plan, decision or conclusion: a pre-mortem, falsification against competing explanations, and a dialectic steelman. It needs no other skill; the skills it mentions are optional complements.

Version 1.1.0 adds one check to falsification: before writing that something exists or does not, look at the source.

Acquire the whole `critical-thinking` directory from `Tacuchi/agent-skills` once the ref `skill/critical-thinking/v1.1.0` exists.
